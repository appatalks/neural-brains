# Relay uncertainty: matched inputs and held-out validation

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Result:** matched neural controls pass; the selected
relay **FAILED independent validation:25/26checks**.
The old [failed relay experiment](brain-spike-joint-test.md) remains failed.
No normal gait, app controller or default relay parameters were changed.

## Frozen question and hypotheses

Can a bounded change in the **assumed** spike-to-current transfer preserve
motor recruitment and FG-dependent suppression across different genuine
brain spike patterns, without fitting against the held-out cases?

[test_relay_sensitivity.py](../scripts/test_relay_sensitivity.py) saved the
full protocol and code hashes before neural exports or candidate evaluation.
Development brain seeds were420/421. New held-out seeds431/432 were not
generated until the selection file had been frozen.

Nine hypotheses cross:

- Mean current at100Hz:125,250 or500model units, half/equal/double the published
  tonic-drive comparison. These are not measured membrane currents.
- Relay decay:20,40 or80ms, around the published20ms rate-cell time scale and
  the previous40ms transmission assumption.
- Per-spike gain = mean current / (100Hz * decay), preserving the declared
  steady-mean normalization while varying pulse shape.

All VNC rate parameters, recruitment, physical torque/reflex gains, fixture,
stimulus rates/windows and mechanical bounds stayed fixed.

## Matched external neural stimulation

The optional matched mode of
[export_bdn2_packets.py](../scripts/export_bdn2_packets.py) constructs a
seeded Bernoulli approximation to150Hz Poisson activation on a0.1ms grid.
There are twelve BPN and two FG external activation channels, using the
same pinned model activation weight/refractory rule.
The resulting external input events are synthetic research stimulation;
the **brain readout spikes are still generated by the actual127,400-neuron
FlyWire LIF network**, not filled in or manually specified.

The BPN event stream is identical across the relevant conditions.
FG-coactivation and FG-output-cut use **exactly the same BPN+FG event array**.
The latter only zeros FG's recurrent chemical outputs: FG still receives
input and fires. Input-event files and packet files have checked hashes.

This removes the previous unmatched shared-RNG stimulation issue. It does
not reproduce the original PoissonInput random stream or model natural
food/sensory encoding. Matched mode is opt-in and never enabled in the app.

Both development and held-out neural exports pass **22/22 controls** each,
including exact sham/reset replay, silent rest, source/BDN silencing,
matched FG event identity and release of inhibition after FG output cut.

| Seed | LeftBDN2 spikes, intact | FG coactivation | Matched FG-output cut |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| development420 | 63 | 43 | 63 |
| development421 | 70 | 47 | 70 |
| held-out431 | 66 | 44 | 66 |

External input identity does not require neural output identity: recurrent
feedback and the intended FG intervention may change firing.

## Development gates and fixed selection

Each candidate had to pass both new matched-input development seeds, plus
the original weak420/421 intact packets as known regression cases.
Those known failures were **development data**, not held-out validation.

Required gates:

1. Paired intact-minus-no-input joint response>0.001rad.
2. Matched FG coactivation has fewer delivered source spikes, lower VNC motor
   rate integral and lower physical activation integral than matched FG-output
   cut, over the one-second interval.
3. Same response bound on the legacy420/421 intact packets.
4. Fixture drift<0.01cm, torque<=0.002g*cm^2/s^2 and source joint limits+0.02rad.
5. No VNC motor output before the first delivered neural packet.

The new FG quantity is **integrated recruitment under matched input**, not the
old unmatched global-peak condition. Peak values are retained in case logs,
but the prior62/66 result is not reclassified as passing this new protocol.

The shared no-input bench is computed once and explicitly reused: an exactly
zero source produces exactly zero relay/VNC drive for every positive gain/
decay candidate, while physical parameters and initial conditions are fixed.
This is cached deterministic baseline physics, not a frozen animal pose.

There were72development physical trials, nine candidates times two seeds
times four traces, plus the shared baseline:

| Mean at100Hz | 20ms decay | 40ms decay | 80ms decay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 125 | failed | failed | failed |
| 250 | passed | failed | failed |
| 500 | passed | passed | passed |

The selection rule was fixed before running: among candidates passing every
development gate, minimize log2 distance from250mean/40ms; break ties by lower
mean then shorter decay. It selected:

```text
mean250model units at100Hz
20ms decay
125model-current units per delivered spike
```

This changes temporal shape while retaining the old250mean normalization.
It is not the globally optimal or physiologically measured transfer.
Selection was written before held-out generation, its hash checked afterward,
and no parameters were refitted against431/432.

The selected candidate barely passed one legacy development case:
420paired response0.001014rad versus the0.001rad threshold.
That limited operating margin matters when interpreting validation.

## Independent held-out outcomes

Twenty-four held-out physical cases tested the selected candidate.
Sham/reset, source/relay/motor disconnections, FG controls, feedback removal,
delivery clocks and physical bounds were retained.

| Held-out seed | Maximum paired joint response | Motion gate |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 431 | 0.001233rad | passed |
| 432 | 0.000194rad | **failed** |

**25/26 held-out checks passed.** The sole failed gate is432's minimum
motor-dependent movement. The full experiment remains **FAILED**.

For both held-out seeds:

- Matched FG inhibition reduces VNC motor integral and integrated physical
  activation; FG output disconnection releases that inhibition.
- Sham/reset rate arrays and physical trajectory hashes match exactly.
- No-input, BPN cut, BDN2 clamp, relay cut, VNC motor silence and physical
  motor disconnection match the actual no-input physical trajectory.
- Feedback removal changes joint motion and applies zero local feedback.
- Delivery timing and physical bounds pass. Maximum fixture drift is
  0.001060cm; maximum torque is0.0001384g*cm^2/s^2.

Other development-passing candidates were **not** switched in after seeing
the failed held-out seed. Selecting one on those outcomes would make431/432
part of fitting rather than an independent test.

## Interpretation and next gate

Matched controls resolve the previous input-stream ambiguity and support
the modeled FG-to-motor suppression path under the tested assumptions.
Temporal relay shaping helps the known development cases, but passing a
threshold on that small set does not establish robust recruitment.
The near-threshold selection did not generalize to432.

A subsequent protocol should predeclare a development operating-margin
criterion and a new independent validation set, or obtain physiological
transfer measurements. Any use of431/432 in future fitting must explicitly
reclassify them as development data and reserve fresh seeds.
Do not lower the0.001 bound, erase432, or claim a stronger development
candidate is validated without testing it prospectively.

This remains an evoked, offline brain-spike-to-rate-to-joint surrogate on an
external fixture. Muscle anatomy, central proprioceptive feedback, multi-leg
coordination, natural sensing and spontaneous/autonomous gait remain missing.
